      How the score is worked out

      01

      Extract

      pdf.js reads the text layer of every page in the browser and joins it into one lowercase string. No text layer (a scanned image) means nothing to score.

      02

      Match

      Each skill in the dictionary has aliases — JavaScript, JS, ES6 — and is looked for as a whole word, so "Java" never matches "JavaScript".

      03

      Weigh

      Core skills carry more weight than tooling. The score is the weight you cover divided by the total weight, as a percentage.
